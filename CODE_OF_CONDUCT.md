# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Anime Array exists so that anime fans can talk about anime with something that answers back. That only works if people can do it without being harassed, and that only works if everyone taking part agrees to some rules.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

---

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being patient with people who are learning, including people learning to code
- Being generous with corrections, especially factual ones
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by a mistake, and learning from it
- Focusing on what is best for the project and for the people using it
- Being curious rather than cynical about a technical disagreement

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form
- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Trolling, inflammatory comment bait, and sustained disruption of discussion
- Publishing others' private information — a physical or email address, a private conversation, a saved chat history — without explicit permission
- Deliberately provoking other characters or users to produce content that breaches the [Content Policy](docs/policies.html), including sexual content involving minors and content exploiting a real person
- Sustained disruption of the platform's operation
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces — this repository, the published documentation site, the [contact form](https://animearray.site/contact-us/), the [AI Hub](https://animearray.site/ai-hub/) and any character request thread — and also applies when somebody is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Character content versus conduct

This needs stating separately, because it is the most likely source of confusion on this project.

**Characters are rude, and that is the product.** Levi answers in short sentences because that is who he is. Vegeta is dismissive because that is his personality. Sukuna is openly contemptuous and does not care that you dislike it. Naruto will not stop talking. Any of that is a character behaving in character, and it is **not** a code of conduct matter.

**Conduct is how you treat people.** Being deliberately abusive to a person through the platform, targeting somebody with repeated unwanted content, or using the platform to harass an individual is a conduct matter, and it is treated as one regardless of which character delivered it. The full behavioural position is set out in the [Content and IP Policy](docs/policies.html).

The distinction being drawn is between a character staying in character, and a person using the platform to cause harm.

## Reporting

If you see unacceptable behaviour, or are subject to it, report it through the [contact form](https://animearray.site/contact-us/) or to contact@animearray.site.

When reporting, please include:

- what happened, described factually
- when and where it happened — a URL, a character, an account
- who was involved, if you know
- anything that would help establish what occurred

Reports are read by a person. Genuine reports are acted on.

If your report concerns an immediate risk of harm, contact the relevant authorities rather than waiting on a project inbox.

## Enforcement

### What happens

All reports are reviewed. What follows depends on what happened, how severe it is, and whether it has happened before.

| Outcome | When it applies |
|---|---|
| A private correction | A first and minor breach, or a good-faith mistake |
| A warning | A repeated minor breach, or a moderate one |
| A temporary ban | A serious breach, or continued behaviour after a warning |
| A permanent ban | Harassment, threats, exploitation of minors, or repeated serious breaches |

### Principles

**Behaviour over intention.** A well-meaning person who caused harm still caused harm. That does not mean intent is irrelevant to the outcome, but it is not a defence.

**Proportionality.** Someone who said the wrong thing once and someone who has been abusing people for a week do not get the same response, and they are not treated as the same kind of problem.

**Privacy.** A report is handled privately. Anonymous reporting is available where it can be done safely. People are not publicly named as complainants.

**No retaliation.** Retaliating against somebody who has made a good-faith report is itself a serious breach.

**Appeals.** Any decision can be appealed by contacting the same address, and the appeal is looked at by somebody other than the person who made the original decision where that is possible.
