# Brand guidelines

The reference for how Anime Array writes, names things, and makes claims. Written so that anyone can produce copy that sounds like it belongs here without having to guess.

---

## One-line description

> Anime Array is a free, independent, fan-built platform of AI tools for anime fans.

Short enough to be quoted whole, specific enough to be checked. Use this when one sentence is needed.

---

## Longer descriptions

**Medium (about 25 words)**

> Anime Array is an independent platform of free AI tools for anime fans — character roleplay, a conversational anime assistant, a recommendation engine and an image generator. No account required.

**Long (about 60 words)**

> Anime Array is a free, independent, fan-built platform of AI tools for anime fans. Chat with twelve characters who each answer in their own voice, ask Otaku AI about series, characters and lore, get recommendations built from what you actually watched, and generate anime artwork. Every core feature is free, none requires an account, and the platform hosts no video.

**Very long (about 120 words)**

> Anime Array is a free, independent, fan-built platform of AI tools for anime fans. Its four products are character roleplay with twelve distinct anime characters, the Otaku AI conversational assistant with two modes and eight built-in tools, the AniPick AI recommendation engine, and the Vision AI image generator. Every core feature is free, none requires an account, and the site hosts no episodes, films or media files of any kind. It is funded by clearly labelled advertising, does not sell user data, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any anime studio, publisher or licensor.

Pick by context, not by length preference. Longer descriptions include the negative claims — no video, no account, no affiliation — because those are the questions people actually have.

## Voice

### What it sounds like

**Direct.** The first sentence answers the question. Context comes after, not before.

**Specific.** "Twelve characters, each with a distinct voice" beats "many characters to explore". "Three pieces of input" beats "minimal setup".

**Plain.** Short sentences where the meaning allows. No jargon without an immediate definition.

**Honest about limits.** A site asking for trust should volunteer what it cannot do.

**Calm.** Not hyped, not apologetic, not defensive.

### What it does not sound like

| Avoid | Instead |
|---|---|
| "Revolutionary" | Nothing — describe what it does |
| "Dive into" | "Read", "look at", "start with" |
| "Game-changing" | Nothing |
| "Seamless" | Nothing |
| "Unlock" | "Use", "open", "get to" |
| "Exciting" | The actual reason it is worth doing |
| "We're thrilled to announce" | "New:" |
| "Best-in-class" | The measurable fact that supports it |
| "Effortlessly" | Nothing |
| "Robust" or "powerful" | The specific capability |

### Sentence style

Varied length. A long sentence that carries a list, followed by a short one that lands. Never two consecutive paragraphs with identical rhythm.

Contractions are fine and preferred. "Do not" becomes "don't". Exception: the formal voice on the legal and policy pages, where "does not" is deliberate.

## Vocabulary

### Preferred

- **Free** — not "free of charge", not "at no cost"
- **No account required** — not "no sign-up needed" (they are different things, and the accurate one is stronger)
- **Characters** — never "personas" unless referring to user-created ones
- **Answer in their own voice** — not "speaks like the real character"
- **Simulated**, **inspired by**, **original conversational system** — for characters
- **Free with no account** — for products

### Never used

- "Affiliated with" any studio, publisher, licensor, broadcaster or rights holder
- "Official" in any sense
- "Licensed" in any sense
- "Voice of [character]"
- "Powered by" any third party whose name would imply endorsement
- "Official lore", "true to canon" as a claim about a reply
- "Free forever" — a promise nobody can keep
- "100% accurate"

### Capitalisation and naming

- **Anime Array**, capitalised, both words, every time. Never "AnimeArray" in prose — it is acceptable as a handle where a single token is required.
- **Otaku AI**, **AniPick AI**, **Vision AI**, **Anime Array Roleplay**
- **AI Hub** capitalised as a proper noun: "the AI Hub", "listed on the AI Hub"
- Character names in full on first mention, standard franchise spelling thereafter: Gojo Satoru, Ryomen Sukuna, Naruto Uzumaki, Itachi Uchiha, Madara Uchiha, Obito Uchiha, Monkey D. Luffy, Roronoa Zoro, Son Goku, Prince Vegeta, Eren Yeager, Levi Ackerman

## The claims that must always be present

These are not marketing. They are the things people ask about before trusting a site that involves other people's characters, and every About-level description includes them.

1. **Free.** Every core feature.
2. **No account required.** For any core feature.
3. **Hosts no video.** No episodes, films, subtitles or media files.
4. **Not affiliated** with any studio, publisher or licensor.
5. **No voice cloning.** No real person's performance is reproduced.
6. **User data is not sold.**
7. **Character replies are not canon.** They are original responses in a character's voice.

Dropping these is not a style problem. The [Content Policy](content-policy.md) exists because people are owed them.

## Messaging hierarchy

Order matters. Each level answers a different question, and a shorter piece should never skip a level to reach a longer one.

**Level 1 — what is it?**
Free AI tools for anime fans.

**Level 2 — what does it do?**
Chat with twelve characters, ask an anime assistant, get recommendations, generate artwork.

**Level 3 — why is it different?**
The characters actually behave differently. The recommendations come from what you watched, not what sells. No account, no paywall, no video.

**Level 4 — who runs it and on what principles?**
An independent fan project. Tested on mobile data. No dark patterns. Corrections handled as a priority.

**Level 5 — the details.**
Roster, tools, modes, roadmap, funding, accessibility, privacy.

### Anti-patterns

- Starting at Level 3 without Levels 1 and 2. Nobody trusts a differentiator before knowing what the product is.
- Putting Level 5 in an ad. Ad copy is Level 2 at most.
- Leading with funding before leading with function.

## Descriptions for each product

### Anime Array Roleplay

> Hold a real conversation with twelve anime characters, each of whom answers in a distinct voice and behaves differently under pressure. Free, no account required.

Never: "Chat with your favourite characters" — it is generic and says nothing about why these twelve rather than any other twelve.

### Otaku AI

> A conversational anime assistant that knows the lore. Runs in two modes — Standard Brain for direct answers, Character RP for answers in a character's voice — with eight built-in tools including recommendations, what's currently airing, power scaling and philosophy.

### AniPick AI

> A recommendation engine built from what you actually watched, loved and skipped. It weighs the titles you abandoned as heavily as the ones you loved, and gives you something useful in one sitting rather than after months of tracking.

### Vision AI

> An anime image generator for character art, wallpapers and creative work. Original outputs, not reproductions of existing animation, manga or artwork.

### AI Hub

> Every Anime Array tool on one page, plus the place to request new characters.

## Meta descriptions

The rule, in order: what it is, then what it does, then what removes the objection.

Length: 120–160 characters. Under 110 wastes space; over 160 truncates.

**Good — 154 characters:**

> Anime Array is a free platform of AI tools for anime fans. Chat with 12 characters in their own voice, ask Otaku AI about the lore, find your next series.

**Bad — 193 characters, truncates:**

> Anime Array is a free, independent platform of AI tools for anime fans. Chat with 12 characters who answer in their own voice, ask Otaku AI about the lore, and find your next series. No signup.

**Bad — 61 characters, wastes space:**

> Free AI tools for anime fans. Chat with characters and get anime recommendations.

## Page titles

Pattern: `[Primary thing] — [qualifier] | Anime Array`

- Max 60 characters. Google truncates around 580px, which is roughly there.
- The primary thing goes first. A qualifier that reads well when the title is cut is useless.
- Do not put the brand name first on pages that should rank for something else.

Examples:

```
Anime Array — Free AI tools for anime fans | Chat, Discover, Create
About Anime Array — An independent fan-built AI platform
Anime Array Products — Roleplay, Otaku AI, AniPick, Vision AI
Frequently Asked Questions — Anime Array
```

## Tone under stress

Three moments where tone matters more than usual.

**When something is broken.** Say what is broken, say what is affected, say what is being done, give a time. Do not minimise, do not blame a third party, do not apologise at length. "Character chat is returning errors for about 20 minutes. It is being fixed now. Updates here." is right. Five paragraphs of apology is not.

**When a copyright complaint arrives.** Acknowledge, do not argue, do not litigate over email, do not ask the complainant to justify their own rights. Confirm receipt, say what happens next, follow through. The [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/muqeet-1138/Anime-Array/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Content Policy](content-policy.md) both reflect this.

**When somebody is wrong.** Correct the specific error, thank them if they were trying to help, and fix the underlying thing rather than the individual reply. Do not be defensive about a factual error on a platform whose whole product is character knowledge.

## Links

Link descriptively. "Read the Content Policy", not "click here". The destination tells the reader what they get before they commit to clicking, which matters more on a page with many links than it does on a page with one.

Internal links use the site's own pages. External links to other anime services are avoided entirely — pointing people to where they should watch is the correct behaviour, but doing it as link equity is not, and the two are indistinguishable to a crawler.

Never link a download. There is no such thing here.

## Review checklist

Before anything ships:

- [ ] First sentence answers the question in the headline
- [ ] Free, no-account and no-video claims present where the level requires them
- [ ] No affiliation implied anywhere
- [ ] No superlative that is not measurable
- [ ] No "seamless", "revolutionary", "dive into", "unlock", "exciting"
- [ ] Character names correct and capitalised
- [ ] Sentence lengths varied
- [ ] Claims verified, not assumed — every factual statement checkable
- [ ] Any reply presented as original, not as canon
- [ ] No fake reviews, testimonials or social proof
