# Content and intellectual property policy

What may be written about characters, series and rights holders on Anime Array — and what must not. This is the internal standard behind the published [Policies page](../docs/policies.html).

Where this document and the published page differ, the published page and the platform's own [Terms of Service](https://animearray.site/terms-of-service/) govern.

---

## The core position

Anime Array is a fan project built on other people's characters. That creates an obligation to be precise about whose work it is, what has been done with it, and what gets removed on request.

**Anime Array is not affiliated with, endorsed by, licensed by, sponsored by, authorised by or approved by any anime studio, manga publisher, licensor, broadcaster, distributor, streaming service or rights holder.**

This is stated everywhere, without exception, and is not softened with "inspired by", "in partnership with", "featuring" or "proudly presenting". Those phrases imply a relationship that does not exist.

---

## Four absolute prohibitions

These are not guidelines. They are the line.

### 1. No protected media, ever

Not hosted. Not uploaded. Not embedded. Not mirrored. Not linked for download. Not shown behind a click. Not screenshotted into a social card.

- No animation. No video, episode, film, trailer or clip.
- No manga. No panel, page, spread or illustration.
- No audio. No voice performance, soundtrack, sound effect or subtitle track.
- No script or prose. No dialogue, narration or passage from any script, novel, light novel or translation.
- No images of any of the above.

This holds even where material is widely shared, even where a rights holder has elsewhere taken a permissive position on a specific work, and even where the material appears in search results for the site. Individual permission for one thing is not a general licence.

### 2. No voice cloning

No character's dialogue is spoken by a real person in real time. No voice actor's performance is reproduced, sampled, converted or imitated. No audio synthesis of any performer is offered anywhere on the platform.

Voice actors have not participated in, authorised or endorsed this project, and any copy implying otherwise is prohibited.

### 3. No affiliation claims

Never write, and never let anybody write:

- "Official" in any sense
- "Licensed by" or "in partnership with" or "in collaboration with"
- "Approved by" or "endorsed by"
- "Featuring characters from [studio]"
- "The [studio] family of characters"

Naming a studio to identify which series a character comes from is fine. The sentence must make clear that the studio is the *source*, not a *partner*.

- **Fine:** "Gojo Satoru — Jujutsu Kaisen"
- **Not fine:** "Gojo Satoru, the official Jujutsu Kaisen character on Anime Array"

### 4. No misrepresentation of generated output

Generated images and generated replies must never be presented as:

- a real quote from any script, novel, panel or dub
- an official statement by a rights holder
- the opinion of a character as expressed by their creator
- a real person's view, words or likeness

Generated output is original output. Say so.

## Character replies are not canon

This needs stating separately because it is the point most likely to be misunderstood, including by people who are trying to be fair.

A character reply from Anime Array is **an original conversational response generated in a character's voice**. It is not a quotation and not a factual claim.

Where a character is given a situation the source material never covers — which happens constantly, because the point is that you can ask them anything — the reply is reasoned from that character's temperament.

Required handling:

| Situation | Do | Do not |
|---|---|---|
| Describing a character's personality | "Levi answers in short sentences and rarely elaborates" | "Levi would tell you to deal with it" as a factual claim |
| Reporting a reply | "Asked X, the character replied Y" | "According to [source], the character says Y" |
| Presenting a reply as canon | Never | "In the source material, the character explains…" |
| A reply contradicts canon | Correct the underlying knowledge, keep the character in character | Alter the character's personality to match a fact about them |

The distinction being drawn: a character can stay in character while stating something factually wrong about their own series, and that is a knowledge bug, not a character bug. Fixing it means correcting what the system knows, not flattening the personality.

## Factual accuracy obligations

The product rests on knowledge about existing series, so accuracy is a content obligation rather than a nice-to-have.

**Corrections are the highest-priority content task.** Somebody who notices a wrong fact and reports it is doing the most valuable thing available to the project, and that requires no technical skill.

Every report must be handled per the platform's [Editorial Guidelines and Corrections Policy](https://animearray.site/editorial-guidelines-and-corrections-policy/): acknowledge, verify, fix the underlying knowledge, confirm.

What is **not** required:

- Agreement with the reporter. If a report is wrong, say so and explain why.
- Immediate agreement to a demanded behaviour change. Requests are assessed against the product's purpose.

## Rights holder requests

Treat every rights holder communication as time-sensitive and high priority. Priority over feature requests, over bug reports, over everything except an active outage.

**What to do:**

1. Acknowledge receipt within 72 hours.
2. Do not argue. Do not require proof of ownership over email before removing anything.
3. Do not litigate over email. If it escalates, escalate properly and calmly.
4. Remove or change what was asked, within what is technically possible.
5. Record what happened so the same request is not mishandled twice.
6. If a pattern of requests emerges, change the platform behaviour rather than handling each instance individually.

**What not to do:**

- Argue about fair use, transformative purpose or the fact that other fan sites do it too
- Ask the complainant to prove they own the work before taking anything down
- Delay in the hope the request goes away
- Delete a valid complainant's own account as a way of resolving their complaint

If something genuinely must remain because it is lawful and necessary, say so plainly, explain why in one sentence, and state what has been changed. Vagueness in this conversation is what turns a complaint into an escalation.

## Acceptable use

Character content that is objectionable because a character is written as they are — Levi terse, Vegeta proud, Sukuna contemptuous, Naruto relentless — is **intended behaviour**, not a violation. See the Code of Conduct's "Character content versus conduct" section.

Conduct that is a violation, regardless of which character was used:

- Harassment, threats or abuse directed at a person
- Sexual content involving minors
- Content exploiting, sexualising or fabricating a real person
- Attempts to circumvent rate limits or access controls for advantage
- Sustained automated scraping that degrades the platform
- Bulk republication of platform design or documentation beyond what the licence permits
- Presenting generated output as real quotes, real views or official statements

## Writing about Anime Array

Required in every About-level description:

1. **Free** — every core feature
2. **No account required** — for any core feature
3. **Hosts no video** — no episodes, films, subtitles or media files
4. **Not affiliated** with any studio, publisher or licensor
5. **No voice cloning**
6. **User data is not sold**
7. **Replies are not canon**

Prohibited:

- Fake reviews, testimonials, ratings or social proof of any kind. Nothing untrue gets written down, regardless of how much it would help.
- Impersonating a studio, publisher, news outlet or a real person
- Claiming endorsement, review or approval that does not exist
- Copyright-encumbered imagery, including in social cards and screenshots
- Suggesting affiliation through imagery, layout or framing rather than words

See [Brand guidelines](brand.md) for the voice and [AI visibility](ai-visibility.md) for how the content is structured so it can be quoted accurately.
